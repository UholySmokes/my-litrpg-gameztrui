# Gold Sinks (Design)

1) **Crafting / enhancement fees**  
   - Gold cost per craft/enhance roll; higher tiers scale cost.

2) **Repairs & durability**  
   - Pay gold to restore durability after combat runs.

3) **Respec / training / rituals**  
   - Gold cost to respec attributes/skills or buy training sessions.

4) **Taxes / permits / titles**  
   - Periodic upkeep or one-time permits for access to services/areas; title purchases.

5) **Consumable upkeep**  
   - Potions/scrolls/food with steady demand; can price by tier and rarity.

Integration targets: `shopData` services, crafting/enhancement UI, title purchases, and future durability system.
