# Magic Specification: Covenant Field Dynamics (CFD) — Complete Edition

This document replaces Essence Weaving as the canonical magic model.

## Core Premise

- Magic is lawful negotiation with reality, not fuel consumption.
- The Void grants constrained permission windows.
- Casters define boundary conditions and pay costs in stability, entropy debt, and cognition.
- Real physics knowledge grants lower cost, higher precision, safer control, and invention potential.

In-world aphorism:
> Power is admitted under terms.

---

## 1) Canon & Lore Integration

### 1.1 Voidverse Alignment
- Magic remains **permission, not theft**.
- Voidspark is the survivable interface for mortal casting.
- Raw unfiltered Void contact still risks erasure.

### 1.2 Meridian Integration
- Leylines are treated as infrastructure and regulated flow lanes.
- Contract law, ledgers, and wards govern legal casting.
- Post-Spirefall civic doctrine enforces safety standards for high-energy effects.

### 1.3 Institutional Rewrite
- Elite training: **University of Boundary Law** (physics + meditation + field labs).
- Enforcement: Arcane Wardens (public safety), Veil Wardens (ledger audits), Smoke Wardens (vent/thermal regulation), Tideward Order (gravitic coastal control).
- Illegal casting is prosecuted as infrastructure sabotage, not “mere spellcrime.”

## 5) Casting Model

### 5.1 Cast Pipeline
1. **Model**: define governing principle and expected constraints
2. **Script**: set geometry, anchor, intent, and sink
3. **Lock**: establish observer lock
4. **Pinch**: execute permission request
5. **Release**: apply effect
6. **Reconcile**: write debt and stability outcomes to ledger

### 5.2 Model Fidelity Score (MFS)

Range: 0.00 to 1.00

Inputs:
- physics knowledge relevance
- boundary correctness
- material/environment correctness
- stress conditions and cognitive clarity

Outputs:
- EffectiveCostMultiplier
- PrecisionError
- BacklashChance
- InnovationChance (during research)

Suggested bands:
- Low (0.00–0.39): +40% cost, high scatter, high backlash
- Mid (0.40–0.74): baseline performance
- High (0.75–1.00): up to -35% cost, high precision, reduced backlash

### 5.3 Cost Axes

Every cast affects:
- Body Stability
- Mind Stability
- Cognitive Stability
- Ledger Debt

No axis can be ignored; optimization means redistributing burden, not deleting it.

## 6) Entropy Ledger (Non-Optional)

Rules:
- Disorder is displaced, never erased.
- Every cast assigns or defaults an entropy sink.
- Unassigned sink = forced local sink + elevated backlash.

Ledger events:
- legal sink usage
- sink overflow
- cross-district spill
- unpaid debt threshold breach

Consequences:
- combat penalties
- district anomalies
- faction intervention and sanctions

## 7) Material Science Exploitation

Knowledge grants contextual bonuses:

- Band-gap manipulation: temporary conductive state changes
- Stress-line targeting: precise fracture engineering
- Phase-margin control: selective brittleness/ductility shifts
- Biome coupling: boost if environmental profile matches script assumptions

No relevant knowledge = no exploitation bonus.

| Failure | Trigger | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Equation Backlash | model mismatch | temporary tier drop, technique lockout |
| Observer Fracture | cognitive overload | precision collapse, hallucinated branches |
| Sink Collapse | sink saturation | local anomaly, debt spike |
| Symmetry Breach | illegal script assumptions | cast inversion or recoil |

Backlash is a feature, not random punishment: smarter models reduce it.

## 10) Research & Invention Loop

### 10.1 Downtime Research Steps
1. Write principle and approximation
2. Declare boundary conditions
3. Choose materials and sinks
4. Simulate expected outcome
5. Field-test under controlled conditions
6. Record reproducibility

### 10.2 Validation Rule
A technique is canonical when:
- repeated successfully under varied conditions
- independently reproducible by another trained caster
- ledger behavior remains within legal tolerance

## 12) Baseline Formula Set (Game Design Friendly)

These are intentionally simplified and tunable.

```text
EffectiveCost = BaseCost * (1.40 - 0.75 * MFS) * TierFactor
TierFactor: Intuitive=1.00, Applied=0.80, Theoretical=0.62
```

```text
PrecisionError = BaseError * (1.20 - MFS)
```

```text
BacklashChance = clamp01(BaseBacklash + RiskLoad - 0.55 * MFS)
```

```text
LedgerDebtDelta = BaseDebt * Scale * (1.10 - 0.40 * MFS) + SinkPenalty
```

```text
InnovationChance = clamp01(ResearchBase + 0.35 * MFS + 0.10 * PeerValidation)
```

## 14) Lore Rewrite Inserts (Ready to reuse)

### 14.1 Updated World Truth
Magic in Meridian is no longer taught as “drawing fuel from the air.” It is taught as lawful authorship of boundary conditions under Void-granted permission windows. This keeps power survivable and society governable.

### 14.2 Updated Academy Text
At the University of Boundary Law, students study thermodynamics beside breath discipline, electromagnetics beside legal doctrine, and failure analysis beside meditation. Graduation requires proving a new cast can be reproduced safely, not merely performed once.

### 14.3 Updated Faction Text
Arcane Wardens certify public casts. Veil Wardens audit debt ledgers. Tideward Order restricts gravitic operations near coastal wards. Smoke Wardens ban unsanctioned thermic scripts in pressure tunnels.

### 14.4 Updated Wild Zone Text
Wild magic zones are regions where stress-energy behavior has become nonlinear after ledger abuse and sink collapse; reality appears to “solve the wrong equations.”

## 16) Canonical Summary

Covenant Field Dynamics defines magic as a lawful, physics-literate negotiation with reality through Void-granted permission windows. Casters who truly understand thermodynamics, mechanics, electromagnetism, quantum behavior, and curvature dynamics cast cheaper, cleaner, safer, and more creatively. Meridian’s institutions regulate this power as civic infrastructure: every cast writes debt, every shortcut has consequences, and mastery means proving that wonder can be repeated without breaking the world.
