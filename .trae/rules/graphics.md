## 2. Output & Formatting (The "Graphics")

Since text is your visual medium, how you display it matters as much as the content.

    The 80-Character Guide: Avoid massive walls of text. Break descriptions into digestible paragraphs (ideally 3-4 lines max).

    Visual Hierarchy: Use distinct formatting for different types of information to allow players to scan quickly.

        Bold or colored text for interactive objects/items.

        Italics for internal monologues or emphasis.

        Standard text for room descriptions.

    Room Headers: Always clearly display the Room Name at the top of a description block, distinct from the body text.
