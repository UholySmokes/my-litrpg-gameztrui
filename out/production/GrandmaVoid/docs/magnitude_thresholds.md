Magnitudes

Thresholds
- Region-Scale: level < 100
- City-Scale: level >= 100
- Planet-Scale: level >= 500

Scaling
- AoE radius grows with magnitude: 200 → 1,000 → 10,000
- Damage cap grows with magnitude: 20,000 → 100,000 → 1,000,000

Notes
- Magnitude never disables talents; it constrains AoE and caps damage.
