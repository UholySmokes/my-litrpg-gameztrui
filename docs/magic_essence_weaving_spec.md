# Magic Specification: Covenant Field Dynamics (CFD) — Complete Edition

**Document Status**: Canonical replacement for Essence Weaving. Fully expanded with mechanics, commands, progression, examples, faction integration, and lore rewrites.

**Core Philosophy**: Magic rewards physics knowledge through efficiency, precision, synergies, and invention. Deeper understanding = lower cost, higher control, novel applications.

**In-world Framing**: "Power is admitted under terms." The Void grants permission windows; casters author boundary conditions. Factions regulate via permits and civic doctrine.

## 1) Lore Integration & Canon Rewrites

### 1.1 Voidverse Cosmology Fit
- Magic is **permission**, not theft (VOIDVERSE_ORIGIN.md).
- Voidspark = survivable seed for observer lock.
- Rewritten: Raw Void-energy erases; CFD casters negotiate lawful field manipulations.

### 1.2 Shardfall Meridian Context (COMPLETE_LORE_COMPENDIUM.md)
- Leylines = regulated permission conduits.
- Factions (Arcane Wardens, Veil Wardens, Smoke Wardens) enforce boundary standards and entropy sinks.
- University of Boundary Law trains Theoretical casters.
- Spirefall = catastrophic permission cascade failure.

### 1.3 Faction/Legal Integration
- **Permit System**: Major effects require faction stamps (e.g., Tideward Order for coastal gravitic).
- **Ledger Audits**: Veil Wardens track entropy debt; unpaid = bounty.
- **Civic Doctrine**: Smoke Wardens ban thermic in vents; violations = quarantine.
